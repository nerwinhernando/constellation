# Plan

## Purpose

A Plan is the root planning aggregate.

Everything related to planning belongs to a Plan.

Future child entities include:

- PlanSection
- Task
- Milestone
- Comment
- Activity

## Rules

Every Plan belongs to one Workspace.

Plans are collaboration objects.

Plans are never shared outside a Workspace.

Plan status:

- draft
- active
- completed
- archived

Plans may be archived but not deleted in normal operation.
